The 3-Tier engine flags these pairs instantly in $< 1\text{ms}$ via sliding-window velocity checks in Redis.

4. Interbank Corridor Surveillance & Regulatory Action:

Under Bank of the Lao PDR (BOL) directives, member compliance officers are advised to enforce Step-Up Authentication (Biometrics / 2FA) on transfers exceeding daily velocity thresholds and report confirmed mule destinations to the LAPNet National Watchlist.
